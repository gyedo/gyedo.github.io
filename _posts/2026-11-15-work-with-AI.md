title: Where the Work Is Divided Between AI and Humans
---

Ever since starting my new job, I've been using AI more than ever before for my daily tasks at work. Naturally, I've been thinking about where the division lies between AI and humans.

Since AI is more capable of generating lines of code than I am, and I've basically been working as a QA person for the output AI makes, I sometimes feel like I'm not contributing enough to my role.

Recently, though, I've started to realize: 
I am the one responsible for what my AI agents produce. 
I'm the one who signs off on that work.

On top of that, given the nature of what I do, I can't work in isolation—there are many people from different disciplines collaborating to achieve our team's shared goal.

As a human, I'm in charge of communicating and discussing the direction and requirements for the code AI generates. I'm also the one who listens to different opinions, yields to ideas better than mine, and sometimes persuades others to get on board with mine.

When considering all of this, what humans are doing in this AI era might not be all that different from pre-AI times.

We are the ones who work together.
We are the ones who make decisions.
We are the ones who take responsibility.

Because of this, soft skills may be the most important things we need to develop to succeed today, and a willingness to work as a team is the spirit we all need to share.
